## Common Problems And How To Fix Them

The two questions that come up most in real user discussions about shopback shopee are: "my cashback didn't track, what do I do?" and "the cashback amount is way smaller than I expected."

### Missing Cashback

If your Shopee order doesn't show up in your ShopBack activity within 48 hours, you can file a missing-cashback claim. The window is 30 days from the purchase date — after that, the option disappears. In the app, go to Account → Cashback → Missing Cashback, or use the Support page and select "Request Missing Cashback." You'll need your order details: the store, the date, the order number, and the amount.

The most common causes, based on what users report and what ShopBack's own documentation says: ad-blockers or privacy extensions on the checkout page, a competing cashback extension (like Honey or Rakuten's button) firing at the same time, clicking a Facebook or Google ad between ShopBack and checkout, or using a promo code that didn't come from ShopBack's page. The fix is to disable other extensions on the Shopee checkout page, clear your cart before starting, and complete checkout in one uninterrupted session.

### Smaller-Than-Expected Cashback

This is almost always the "indirect order" problem. You landed on one Shopee seller through ShopBack but bought from a different seller, or you did a generic Shopee search instead of searching for the specific seller. The result is the 0.1% rate instead of the featured rate. The fix is the habit from earlier: search for the specific seller inside ShopBack, tap through, and buy from that exact seller.

A smaller but real factor: cashback is calculated on the purchase subtotal, not on shipping, taxes, or service charges. A $100 order with $10 shipping returns cashback on $100, not $110. Most stores exclude shipping and taxes from the calculation.
